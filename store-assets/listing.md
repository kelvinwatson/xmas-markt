# Google Play listing text

## English (en-GB)

**Title (≤30):** XmasMarkt: Berlin Xmas Markets
**Short description (≤80):** Every Christmas market in Berlin on one map – dates, hours and entry.

**Full description:**
XmasMarkt shows the Christmas markets across Berlin on a single map, so you can see what is near you and plan your evenings.

• Map and list – browse all markets on the map or as a list with photos
• Dates, opening hours and entry (free or ticketed) as published by visitBerlin.de
• Honest about what isn't known yet – markets whose dates are not announced are marked as pending instead of guessed
• Filter by district and entry, sort by date or name
• Save your favourite markets to a personal list
• Add a market to your calendar or share it with friends
• English and German, dark and light theme
• Data refreshed automatically every day

No account, no ads. Anonymous, cookie-free usage statistics only. Location is used only if you tap "locate me" and never leaves your device.

Market data and photos: visitBerlin.de. Please check the organiser's own site before you go – dates and hours can change.
